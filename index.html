Media roadmap: H2 2026 and 2027

PM: Maj Askew As of 2026-10-14 Draft v2 Shared capability across Lodging, Reviews & Destinations
Timeline H2 2026 2027

Images move behavior faster than any other content type, and most of the funnel hasn't seen the improvements we already know how to deliver. Four quality pillars organize the work.

Completeness
Properties and destinations have the images, video, amenities and content travelers need.
Appeal
Images are well presented and demonstrably effective.
Relevance
The right image for the right traveler, surface and trip.
Accuracy
We display images that meet our standards and represent what is intended, and remove those that don't.
Timeline

Delivered work spans H2 2026 so far. Open work is drawn from today forward. Placement beyond Q4 is a working draft.
